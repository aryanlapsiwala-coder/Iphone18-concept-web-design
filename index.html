New dimensions in power.

Whether you're working with intensive graphics or massive media files, iPhone 18 offers blazing-fast performance with advanced cooling. An Apple-designed vapour chamber works with the titanium unibody to efficiently move heat away from the A20 Pro chip, allowing for even higher sustained performance. This breakthrough in thermal management is key to the most powerful iPhone ever created.
