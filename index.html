Performance

New dimensions in power.

Whether you're working with intensive graphics or massive media files, iPhone 18 offers blazing-fast performance with advanced cooling. An Apple-designed laser-welded vapour chamber works with the titanium unibody to efficiently move heat away from the A20 Pro chip, allowing for even higher sustained performance*. This breakthrough in thermal management is key to the most powerful iPhone ever created.

A20 Pro chip
The Apple silicon powering iPhone 18 delivers the highest iPhone performance ever, ideal for advanced gaming and the most demanding tasks.
Graphics and speed
